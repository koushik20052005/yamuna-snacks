Our Story

Yamuna Snacks is a small family stall started by grandma and uncle in Nagole. Grandma's recipes, uncle's stall, and the same Telangana tastes every evening — crispy Sarva Pindi pressed fresh, chatpata Muntha Masala, hot Bread Omelette, soft Ponganalu and Egg Pouch. Simple food, made with care, at honest prices.
